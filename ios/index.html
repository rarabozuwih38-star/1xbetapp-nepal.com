1xBet iOS App Nepal: The Apple Path Explained

The official workaround is straightforward: temporarily switch your Apple ID region to a country where the app is listed (Cyprus is the most common choice), install, then switch back if you prefer.

Why 1xBet Is Hidden in the Nepal App Store

Apple's App Review Guideline 5.3 covers gambling apps. The rule: an app may list real-money gambling only in storefronts where the app holds a verified local licence. 1xBet operates under a Curaçao master licence — accepted by Cyprus, Egypt, and several other regions for storefront listing, but not by Apple's Nepal storefront, which has no formal gambling-listing framework.

This is purely a catalogue-visibility decision. The app itself is identical across regions. Once installed, the app talks to 1xBet's global servers and behaves the same regardless of which storefront you originally downloaded from.

1xBet iOS Apple ID Region Switch Step by Step

The switch takes around two minutes. You will need an internet connection and your Apple ID password. No payment method change is required — Cyprus accepts "None" as billing.

  1. Open Settings → tap your Apple ID name at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap "Media & Purchases" → View Account. Enter your Apple ID password or use FaceID if prompted.
  3. Tap "Country/Region" → "Change Country or Region". A list of countries appears.
  4. Select Cyprus. Egypt and Mexico work too, but Cyprus has the simplest billing requirement.
  5. Accept the Apple Media Services Terms. A long legal text — scroll, tap Agree.
  6. Payment method screen. Select "None". Apple does not enforce a paid card for browsing free apps. Skip the billing address (any city in Cyprus is accepted, e.g. Nicosia).
  7. Region switch confirms. The App Store now reflects Cyprus catalogue.
  8. Open App Store, search "1xbet". The official app (blue logo, publisher "1XCorp N.V.") appears as the first result. Tap Get.
  9. Install completes. Open the app, pick Nepal as your country, NPR as currency.

Switching Apple ID Region Back to Nepal

After install, you can revert your Apple ID to Nepal. The 1xBet app stays installed and fully functional. To switch back: Settings → Apple ID → Media & Purchases → View Account → Country/Region → choose Nepal. The same payment-method-None workflow applies.

What Stays Intact When You Switch Apple ID Region

A common worry: switching region might wipe iCloud data or break existing app subscriptions. It does not. Here is what is and is not affected:

Unchanged After Region Switch

  • iCloud Photos, Notes, Reminders, Mail, Contacts.
  • Find My iPhone, Find My Friends.
  • iMessage, FaceTime, Apple ID itself (same email, same password).
  • Apple Watch pairing and watchOS data.
  • All non-store data (saved Safari tabs, Wallet boarding passes, Health data).
  • Already-installed apps from other regions — they keep working.

Changes With Region Switch

  • App Store catalogue: reflects new region's available apps.
  • Music store catalogue (Apple Music): some songs/albums may show "unavailable in this region" temporarily.
  • iCloud storage subscription billing: needs to be cancelled in the original region before switching, or it stays active under the old country.
  • Apple Pay cards added in Nepal: keep working for in-store payments. Only Apple Pay through App Store would be affected.

If you have an active Apple One subscription or iCloud+ plan, cancel it 24 hours before the region switch and re-add it after. Otherwise the billing system gets confused (not damaged, just inconvenient).

1xBet iOS Update Mechanism

App Store auto-update works while your Apple ID region matches a 1xBet-listed country. Once you switch back to Nepal, App Store no longer pushes updates — but the app handles its own version checks. On every launch, the app verifies against 1xBet's CDN and prompts to update in-app when a new build is ready.

  • In-app update prompt: top banner reads "Update available". Tap, the new IPA downloads in-app.
  • Install confirmation: iOS asks for permission once. After that, future updates need only a tap.
  • Update size: delta updates roughly 30 MB on average. Full reinstall is required only on major version jumps (e.g. iOS 16 → iOS 17 platform changes).

1xBet iOS Troubleshooting

  • "Cannot connect to App Store": region is not set to Cyprus (or another supported region). Verify under Settings → Apple ID → Media & Purchases → View Account.
  • App icon shows but won't launch: iOS 16.3 or earlier — update iOS to 16.4 minimum.
  • FaceID stops working after region switch: Open the app → Settings → Security → Biometric Login → toggle off, then on again. iOS re-registers the API binding.
  • "This app is no longer available in your country": Apple ID switched back to Nepal mid-install. Switch to Cyprus, retry.
  • Live stream stutters on cellular: Settings → Cellular → 1xBet → toggle off "Low Data Mode" if it was enabled system-wide.
  • Apple Pay button hidden: expected behaviour. eSewa/Khalti work for all deposits.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why isn't 1xBet in the Nepal App Store?

A: Apple's regional App Store policy gates real-money gambling apps based on each country's regulatory profile. Nepal's App Store does not list 1xBet, but Cyprus, Egypt, and several other regions do. Switching the Apple ID region temporarily allows install.

Q: Does switching Apple ID region risk losing iCloud data?

A: No. iCloud Photos, Notes, Mail, Find My, and all data remain intact. Only the App Store catalogue and billing methods change. You can switch back to Nepal at any time without affecting your installed 1xBet app.

Q: What iOS version is required?

A: iOS 16.4 or newer is required for the current 2026 release. iPhone 8 and later support this version. Older devices can download the legacy build via 1xBet customer support email.

Q: Does FaceID work the same as on the Android app?

A: Yes. Biometric data sits in Apple's Secure Enclave and is never sent to 1xBet servers. iOS handles the API call; the app receives only an "authenticated" or "failed" result. This is more secure than password authentication because it resists phishing.

Q: Can I pay with Apple Pay?

A: Apple Pay is not available for 1xBet deposits in Nepal because Apple's in-app purchase policy blocks gambling transactions. eSewa, Khalti, and IME Pay remain the fastest local options.

Q: Will the app auto-update after I switch back to Nepal region?

A: Auto-update through the App Store stops when you switch back to a region where the app is not listed. The app itself shows an in-app update prompt and downloads new versions silently, so manual updates are not required.
